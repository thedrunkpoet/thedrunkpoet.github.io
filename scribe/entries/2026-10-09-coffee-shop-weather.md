title: Coffee Shop Weather
date: 2026-10-09
mood: soft
---

The table by the window has a wobble. I fixed it with a folded receipt and felt, absurdly, like a person who has their life together.

Overheard: "I just think you can love someone and still not want to see them." Wrote it on my hand. It's still there, smudged, like a bad tattoo of a true thing.

Autumn arrived without asking. Everything smells of cinnamon and wet leaves and other people's plans.
