I have eleven notebooks. Each is about a third full. The first third of anything is easy because it is all promise.

> The middle is where you find out what you actually wanted.

I keep leaving before the middle. This week I'm trying something stupid and small: finish **one** page. Not a good page. A finished one.

## Rules

1. One page.
2. No crossing out the first line.
3. Stop when the page ends, even mid-sentence.
