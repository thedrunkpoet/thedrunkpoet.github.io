It rained all day, the kind of rain that doesn't fall so much as *hang about*. I kept the window open an inch to hear it.

Things I noticed, in no order:

- the kettle takes exactly as long as one good thought
- someone downstairs is learning the cello, badly, with great courage
- the radiator ticks like it's counting something

> Nothing happened today, and I was there for all of it.

## Evening

Made soup. Burned the toast. Ate both anyway, standing up, like a person with nowhere to be.

Tomorrow I'll probably want to do something *meaningful*. Tonight I'm content to have been rained on, indirectly, through a window.
