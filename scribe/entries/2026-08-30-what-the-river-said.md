---
title: What the River Said
date: 2026-08-30
mood: wistful
---

Sat by the river until the light went orange and then grey.

It said nothing, which is what rivers are best at. I admire that. I have spent most of my life narrating, and the river just *goes*.

Maybe journalling is how I practise the river: let it move through, don't hold it.

Left a stone on the wall. Don't ask.
