---
title: The Last Bus Home
date: 2026-11-02
mood: restless
---

Missed the 11:40 by about ten seconds. Watched the tail lights go soft in the rain and decided, stubbornly, that walking was a *choice* and not a defeat.

Forty minutes later I understood why people take buses.

But somewhere around the old bridge the city went quiet in a way it never does at noon. A man was sweeping the steps of a shuttered bakery, slowly, for no one. I thought: *that is a poem, and I am too tired to write it.*

So here is the plain version instead:

- rain on the pavement
- a streetlight with a moth problem
- my own footsteps, arguing

Home by 12:30. Wrote nothing else. Slept well.
