About

You won't find my name anywhere on this site. That's deliberate. It lets me say things I'd otherwise swallow, and it lets the people in these stories stay as unbothered as they deserve to be.

Entries get written late, usually with a glass nearby, and edited barely at all. Expect typos, strong opinions and the occasional poem that seemed better at 2 a.m.

Some entries are funny. Some are sad. A few are both, which is how most of life turns out.

There are no comments, no likes and no sign-ups. Just come in, sit down and read.
